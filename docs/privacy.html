Snooze

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

The short version

  • Snooze keeps your reminders on your phone. You don’t need an account to use it.
  • If you sign in, Snooze also keeps a backup copy of your reminders in your account, so you can get them back on a new phone.
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use your reminders for anything except running Snooze.

What stays on your phone

Your reminders (titles, notes, times, repeat settings and sounds), their history (when you completed, snoozed or missed them) and your settings are stored in the app’s storage on your phone. They don’t leave your phone unless you sign in to back up.

If you sign in to back up

  • Account: your email address and an account ID. If you sign in with Google, also your name and profile picture as Google provides them. Handled by Firebase Authentication (Google).
  • Backup: a copy of your reminders and their history, stored in Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud) under your account ID. It is used only to restore your reminders and keep them the same on your phones.

Crash reports

To find and fix bugs, Snooze sends crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics (Google): what went wrong in the app, your device model, Android version and the app version. They don’t include your reminders.

Permissions

Snooze asks to show notifications, to schedule exact alarms, to show alarms over the lock screen, and to restore alarms after your phone restarts. These only let reminders ring on time; none of them sends data anywhere.

Who handles your data

Google, through Firebase, stores the backup and crash reports for us as our service provider (firebase.google.com/support/privacy). We don’t share your data with anyone else, and we never sell it.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete it or uninstall Snooze.
  • Backup: until you delete your account. When you delete a reminder, its backup copy is replaced by a small marker without its content, so your other phones delete it too.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase for 90 days.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account deletes your account and your backup right away, and can also remove your reminders from the phone.
  • Signing out stops the backup. Your reminders stay on the phone unless you choose to remove them.
  • Without the app: email suryatejap24@gmail.com from your account’s address, and we’ll delete your account and backup within 30 days.

Security

Backups are encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored encrypted by Google Cloud. Security rules let each account read and write only its own backup.

Children

Snooze isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
