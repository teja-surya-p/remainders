**App name** (30 max): Snooze

**Short description** (80 max):
Reminders that ring like an alarm until you answer them. Works offline.

**Full description** (4000 max):

Snooze is for the things you can't afford to miss: medicine, deadlines,
appointments, the call you promised to make.

A Snooze reminder doesn't just buzz once and disappear. It rings like an
alarm, at alarm volume and even on silent, opens full screen over the lock
screen, and keeps going until you answer it: done, or snoozed for 5, 10 or
30 minutes or to a time you pick. If you still don't answer, it's recorded
as missed so nothing slips by unnoticed.

WHY IT'S RELIABLE
• Built around Android's exact alarms, with a setup check that shows what
  could delay or silence your reminders (notifications, alarm permission,
  full-screen alerts, battery saving) and takes you straight to the right
  setting, with steps for Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Oppo, Vivo, Huawei,
  Motorola and Pixel phones.
• Alarms come back after a restart, an app update or a time-zone change.
• Repeats (daily, weekly on chosen days, every few days) are scheduled ahead,
  so they ring even if you don't open the app.

MADE TO BE QUICK
• Today and the week ahead at a glance.
• Snooze or dismiss right from the notification.
• An optional heads-up a few minutes before it rings.
• Follows your phone's 12- or 24-hour clock, light and dark themes.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• No account needed: your reminders stay on your phone.
• Sign in only if you want a backup, to get everything back on a new phone.
• No ads, and your data is never sold.
