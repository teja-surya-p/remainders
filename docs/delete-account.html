Snooze

Delete your Snooze account

Effective November 1, 2026

In the app

1. Open Snooze and go to Profile.

2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

3. Confirm it’s you (Google, or your password).

Your account and its backup are deleted right away. You can also choose to remove your reminders from the phone.

Without the app

Email suryatejap24@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your Snooze account. We’ll delete it within 30 days and reply when it’s done.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, name, account ID).
  • The backup of your reminders and their history.
  • Shared reminders you own; you are removed from others.

What is kept

  • Reminders on your phone stay until you remove them or uninstall Snooze; they were never only in your account.
  • Crash reports contain no account data and expire after 90 days.
