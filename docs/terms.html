Reminders can be missed

Snooze works hard to alert you on time, but your phone decides when apps may run. Battery savers, Do Not Disturb, denied permissions, a phone that is off or out of battery, and system updates can delay or block a reminder.

Don’t rely on Snooze alone for anything where a missed reminder could cause harm, such as critical medication. Follow the setup steps in the app so your phone lets Snooze ring on time.
